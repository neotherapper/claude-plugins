## Unexplored Ideas

### 1. Greek Frontistirio Intelligence Platform
**Model:** Directory + content  
**Domain:** education, greek-market

**What it is:** A comprehensive directory of Greece's frontistiria (private cram schools/supplemental education centers) — the dominant vehicle for Greek exam prep (Panhellenic exams, English certifications, IELTS/Cambridge). Includes ratings, subject/exam coverage, price ranges, and neighborhood filtering.

**Why this matters:**
- Frontistiria are a cornerstone of Greek education culture — virtually every student attends one. The market is enormous but completely unmapped online.
- No directory of frontistiria exists. Parents pick them via word of mouth.
- Natural extension of the Greek Schools Directory data moat — same founder advantage, same audience.
- Potential B2B revenue: frontistiria pay for premium listings (higher conversion intent than school listings).
- SEO goldmine: "φροντιστήρια αθήνα", "φροντιστήριο μαθηματικά θεσσαλονίκη" — high-intent local searches with zero structured results.

**Key questions:**
- How many frontistiria are registered in Greece? (likely 5,000–15,000)
- Are they registered under a Ministry of Education license? If so, data is scrapeable.
- What's the annual spend per student on frontistiria? (estimated EUR 1,000–3,000/yr)

### 2. Greek Panellinies Prep Hub
**Model:** Content + SaaS tool  
**Domain:** education, greek-market

**What it is:** A structured resource platform for the Panhellenic university entrance exams (Πανελλαδικές Εξετάσεις) — including past paper archives, score calculators, cut-off grade trackers by university/department, and a "what university can I get into?" simulator.

**Why this matters:**
- The Panhellenic exams are the single highest-stakes event in a Greek student's life. Demand for prep resources is intense and perennial.
- No clean, well-designed tool exists for score simulation or departmental cut-off tracking. Students use scattered PDFs and outdated government pages.
- Open data: cut-off grades (βαθμολογικές βάσεις) are published annually by the Ministry — scrapeable.
- Traffic model is reliable: same cohort of ~100,000 students takes the exams every June. Predictable demand spike.
- Dovetails with Greek University Finder — same user, one step earlier in their decision journey.

**Key questions:**
- Does a Greek-language equivalent exist (e.g., studium.gr, ei.gr)?
- What's the willingness to pay for a premium calculator vs. free content?
- Can past exam papers be republished legally under Greek/EU open data rules?

### 3. Greek Teacher / Tutor Credential Verifier
**Model:** Tool-site / SaaS  
**Domain:** education, trust, greek-market

**What it is:** A lightweight verification and credentialing layer for private tutors in Greece — allowing tutors to display verified degrees, ASEP (civil service exam) scores, and teaching licenses. Parents get a trust signal; tutors get a credibility badge.

**Why this matters:**
- The EUR 1.8B private tutoring market runs almost entirely on trust signals passed via word of mouth. There is no Upwork-style credential system for Greek tutors.
- Parents hiring tutors for Panhellenic exam prep face real stakes — bad tutor selection costs money and exam performance.
- Could be a standalone product or the trust layer sitting underneath Greek Tutoring Platform / Paidagogos — making those marketplace ideas more defensible.
- ASEP results are public data; degree verification could be self-attested with document upload.

**Key questions:**
- Would tutors pay for a verified badge (EUR 5–20/month)?
- Is there a Greek education equivalent of DBS (UK criminal record check) that parents care about?
- Legal: can ASEP ranking data be republished commercially?

### 4. Greek School Catchment Area & Enrollment Navigator
**Model:** Tool-site  
**Domain:** education, geodata, greek-market

**What it is:** An interactive map tool that answers the question every Greek parent asks when moving: "Which public school is my child assigned to based on our address?" Greek public schools operate on strict catchment zones (σχολικές περιφέρειες) but this data is nowhere findable online in usable form.

**Why this matters:**
- Catchment zone assignment is mandatory and non-obvious — it requires knowing the school zone (σχολική περιφέρεια) for a given address, which varies by municipality.
- The pain is especially acute for: (a) families buying or renting a home and factoring school quality into the decision, (b) Golden Visa expats who don't understand the Greek assignment system.
- This is a direct extension of the Greek Schools Directory data moat — the geodata layer on top of the existing 14,000-school dataset.
- Cross-sells naturally into real estate data (neighborhood intelligence family) — same user researching home + school simultaneously.

**Key questions:**
- Is school catchment boundary data available from municipalities or the Ministry of Education?
- Could this be embedded as a feature of the Greek Schools Directory instead of a standalone product?
- What's the SEO signal for "σχολική περιφέρεια" type queries?

### 5. Greek Language School Directory (for Adults)
**Model:** Directory  
**Domain:** education, greek-market, language-learning

**What it is:** A directory of schools and programs teaching Modern Greek as a foreign language — targeting the growing population of Golden Visa holders, digital nomads, and diaspora members who need to learn Greek (for citizenship applications, daily life, or cultural connection).

**Why this matters:**
- Golden Visa holders (non-EU nationals investing EUR 250K+ in Greek real estate) now have strong incentive to learn Greek for long-term residency and citizenship paths.
- Greek language learning is a growing query internationally but has no structured directory of providers (private schools, university programs like the Athens Centre, online tutors).
- This is a different audience from K-12 (adults, often English-speaking) and monetizes differently (course lead-gen, affiliate revenue from online platforms like Duolingo/Babbel Greek courses).
- Complements Greek Schools Directory without cannibalizing it.

**Key questions:**
- How many Greek language schools exist for adults in Greece?
- Is there an international diaspora segment (Greek-Americans, Greek-Australians) wanting to learn the language?
- What affiliate programs exist for Greek language learning apps?

### 6. Greek Private School Admissions CRM (B2B SaaS)
**Model:** SaaS  
**Domain:** education, b2b, greek-market

**What it is:** A lightweight CRM and admissions management tool designed for Greek private schools — covering inquiry tracking, open day management, application review, and enrollment pipeline. Targets the ~500–1,000 private schools in Greece that currently manage admissions via email and spreadsheets.

**Why this matters:**
- Greek private schools are growing (private university approvals in 2026 signal expanded private sector investment in education).
- Admissions software in Greece is effectively zero — international tools (Veracross, SchoolAdmin) are too expensive and not localized.
- B2B SaaS in a market with 500+ paying schools at EUR 100–500/month = EUR 600K–3M ARR ceiling with a small team.
- Founder advantage: Greek Schools Directory gives direct access to the private school supply side — warm intro to school admissions directors.

**Key questions:**
- Do private Greek schools have dedicated admissions staff or is it handled by the principal?
- What software do they currently use (if any)?
- Is there an GDPR-compliant student data requirement that makes foreign SaaS tools less appealing?

### 7. Greek Education Jobs Board
**Model:** Directory + job board  
**Domain:** education, hr, greek-market

**What it is:** A dedicated jobs board for Greek education — targeting teacher positions at private schools, frontistiria, language schools, and international schools. Includes both Greek-language positions and English-medium roles (for expat teachers).

**Why this matters:**
- Greece has a shortage of qualified English-speaking teachers at international schools. These schools currently recruit via generic boards (LinkedIn) or word of mouth.
- The ASEP (civil servant exam) process covers public school hiring, but private sector teacher hiring has no dedicated platform.
- Founder advantage: direct access to 14,000 school listings means direct outreach to school HR contacts is feasible at scale.
- Revenue model: job listing fees (EUR 100–300 per post) + featured employer packages.

**Key questions:**
- What's the volume of private education job openings in Greece per year?
- Is there a seasonal hiring pattern (summer for September starts)?
- Could this be a feature of Greek Schools Directory B2B product rather than standalone?
