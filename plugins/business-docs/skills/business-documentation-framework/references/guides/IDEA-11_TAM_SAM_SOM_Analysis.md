# Generation Guide: IDEA-11 TAM/SAM/SOM Analysis

**Purpose:** Quantify the market opportunity to understand scale potential.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-09 Lean Canvas

## Questions to Ask the User
- Who is everyone who could possibly use this solution (TAM)?
- What segment can you realistically reach with your current model (SAM)?
- What market share can you capture in 3-5 years (SOM)?
