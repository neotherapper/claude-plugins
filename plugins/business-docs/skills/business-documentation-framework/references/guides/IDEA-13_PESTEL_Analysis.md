# Generation Guide: IDEA-13 PESTEL Analysis

**Purpose:** Analyze macro-environmental factors affecting the market.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-11 Market Analysis

## Questions to Ask the User
- Are there upcoming regulations affecting your industry?
- How do current economic conditions (recession, interest rates) affect your buyers?
- What social or cultural shifts are driving adoption?

## Document Template

```markdown
# PESTEL Analysis

**ID:** IDEA-13
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** IDEA-11
**Gateway:** No

---

## Factors
- **Political:** [Trade policies, government stance on tech]
- **Economic:** [Interest rates, VC funding climate]
- **Social:** [Remote work trends, privacy attitudes]
- **Technological:** [AI advancements, cloud costs]
- **Environmental:** [Sustainability expectations]
- **Legal:** [GDPR, AI regulation]

## Strategic Summary
1. [Highest impact factor] -> [Required action]
2. [Second impact factor] -> [Required action]
```
