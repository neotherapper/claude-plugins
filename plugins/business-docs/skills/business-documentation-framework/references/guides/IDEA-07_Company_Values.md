# Generation Guide: IDEA-07 Company Values

**Purpose:** Define the behavioral principles and cultural standards that guide internal decision-making.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-05 Mission Statement
- IDEA-06 Vision Statement

## Questions to Ask the User
- What behaviors do you value most in team members?
- What are your non-negotiable principles?
- How should the team act when facing a difficult trade-off?
