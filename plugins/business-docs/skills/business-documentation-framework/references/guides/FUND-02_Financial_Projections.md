# Generation Guide: FUND-02 Financial Projections

**Purpose:** Create a 3-5 year financial model showing revenue growth, cost structure, and profitability path.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-09 Lean Canvas
- IDEA-17 Business Case

## Questions to Ask the User
- What is your customer acquisition cost (CAC)?
- What is the expected churn rate?
- What is the monthly burn rate estimate?
