# Generation Guide: IDEA-18 Opportunity Solutions Tree (OST)

**Purpose:** Connect business outcomes to customer opportunities and solution experiments visually.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-09 Lean Canvas
- IDEA-17 OKRs

## Questions to Ask the User
- What measurable outcome are you trying to achieve?
- What customer needs/pains could help achieve this outcome?
- What are the possible solutions for each opportunity?

## Document Template

```markdown
# Opportunity Solutions Tree

**ID:** IDEA-18
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** IDEA-09, IDEA-17
**Gateway:** No

---

## Outcome
> [Specific, measurable goal, e.g., Increase retention by 20%]

## Opportunity Map
### Opportunity 1: [Customer Need]
- **Evidence:** [Interview quote/data]
- **Impact:** [High/Med/Low]

#### Solutions for Opportunity 1
- **Sol A:** [Description] - **Assumptions:** [List]
- **Sol B:** [Description]

## Current Focus
- **Test:** [Active experiment]
```
