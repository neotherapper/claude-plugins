# Generation Guide: IDEA-15 Why Now? Analysis

**Purpose:** Document the specific timing factors that make this the optimal moment for your solution.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-14 Competitive Positioning

## Questions to Ask the User
- What technology shift just made this possible?
- What regulatory change just made this necessary?
- Why was 2 years ago too early, and why is next year too late?

## Document Template

```markdown
# "Why Now?" Analysis

**ID:** IDEA-15
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** IDEA-14
**Gateway:** No

---

## Convergence of Factors
- **Technology Enabler:** [New API, lower compute cost]
- **Market Shift:** [Changing customer behavior]
- **Regulatory Change:** [New law or mandate]
- **Competitive Window:** [Why others missed it]

## Summary Thesis
> [2-3 sentences on the "perfect storm" creating this opportunity]
```
