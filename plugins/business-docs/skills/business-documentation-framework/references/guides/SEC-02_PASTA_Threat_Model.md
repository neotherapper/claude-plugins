# Generation Guide: SEC-02 PASTA Threat Model

**Purpose:** Perform a risk-centric threat modeling process across seven stages to align security with business objectives.

---

## Prerequisites
- TECH-05 C4 Container Diagram

## Questions to Ask the User
- What are the business impacts of a data breach or service outage?
- Who are the likely attacker profiles for this system?
- What are the known vulnerabilities in your technical stack?
