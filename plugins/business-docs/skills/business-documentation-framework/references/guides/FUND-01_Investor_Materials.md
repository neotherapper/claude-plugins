# Generation Guide: FUND-01 Investor Materials

**Purpose:** Consolidate data from market and business model analysis into a format suitable for professional investors.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-16 Pitch Deck
- FUND-02 Financial Projections

## Questions to Ask the User
- What is your cap table structure?
- What are the major milestones for this funding round?
- What is the data room checklist status?
