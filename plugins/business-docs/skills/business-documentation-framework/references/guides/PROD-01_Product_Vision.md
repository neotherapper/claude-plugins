# Generation Guide: PROD-01 Product Vision (GATEWAY)

**Purpose:** Define the "North Star" for the product to guide strategy and execution.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-06 Vision Statement

## Questions to Ask the User
- What does the world look like in 5 years if your product succeeds?
- Who benefits most from your product?
- What will your product NOT do?

## Document Template

```markdown
# Product Vision

**ID:** PROD-01
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** IDEA-06
**Gateway:** Yes

---

## Vision Statement
> [One sentence: The future state the product creates]

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** [Main persona]
- **Value:** [Core benefit]

## Strategic Pillars
1. [Pillar 1]
2. [Pillar 2]

## Success Definition
- **North Star Metric:** [Metric name]
- **Target:** [Value]

## Gateway Approval
**Required:** CPO, CEO
```
