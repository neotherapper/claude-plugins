# Generation Guide: IDEA-08 Value Proposition Canvas

**Purpose:** Map the fit between the customer profile (needs) and the product value map (offerings).

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-04 Problem Statement
- IDEA-02 JTBD Analysis

## Extraction from IDEA-04/02
- Customer segment
- Top jobs, pains, and gains identified in research

## Questions to Ask the User
- What specific gains do your customers want?
- What pains are most severe and frequent?
- What will you specifically offer to address these?
