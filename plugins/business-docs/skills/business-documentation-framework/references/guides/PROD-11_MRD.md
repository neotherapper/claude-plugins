# Generation Guide: PROD-11 Market Requirements Document (MRD)

**Purpose:** Define the market opportunity and competitive environment to justify product development.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-11 TAM/SAM/SOM Analysis
- PROD-20 Competitive Analysis

## Questions to Ask the User
- What specific market segment are you targeting?
- What are the key market trends driving this opportunity?
- What is the primary business case (ROI)?
