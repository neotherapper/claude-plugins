# Generation Guide: IDEA-12 Porter's Five Forces

**Purpose:** Analyze industry attractiveness and competitive intensity.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-11 Market Analysis

## Questions to Ask the User
- How easy is it for new competitors to enter (Threat of Entry)?
- How much power do your customers have over your pricing?
- What are the major alternatives customers could switch to (Substitutes)?
