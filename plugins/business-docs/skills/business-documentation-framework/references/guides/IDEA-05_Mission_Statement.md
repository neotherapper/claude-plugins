# Generation Guide: IDEA-05 Mission Statement

**Purpose:** Define the current purpose of the company—what it does, who it serves, and why.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-04 Problem Statement

## Extraction from IDEA-04
- Problem being solved
- Core target customer segment

## Questions to Ask the User
- What is your company's core action?
- Who are your primary customers?
- What fundamental impact are you trying to achieve right now?

## Document Template

```markdown
# Mission Statement

**ID:** IDEA-05
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** IDEA-04
**Gateway:** No

---

## Our Mission
> [One sentence: What you do, for whom, and why it matters]

## Mission Components
- **What we do:** [Core activity]
- **Who we serve:** [Primary customer segment]
- **Impact we create:** [Direct benefit delivered]

## Guiding Principles
- **Decision lens:** [How this mission helps choose priorities]
- **Product focus:** [How this mission shapes the roadmap]
```
