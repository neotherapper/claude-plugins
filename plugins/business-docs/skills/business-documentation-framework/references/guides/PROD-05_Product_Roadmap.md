# Generation Guide: PROD-05 Product Roadmap (Now-Next-Later)

**Purpose:** Provide a high-level view of the product's direction and timing without making false promises about specific dates.

---

## Prerequisites
- PROD-02 Product Strategy
- PROD-09 PRD

## Questions to Ask the User
- What is being actively built right now?
- What problems are you tackling next (medium confidence)?
- What exploratory themes are on the horizon (long term)?

## Document Template

```markdown
# Product Roadmap

**ID:** PROD-05
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** PROD-02
**Gateway:** No

---

## NOW (In Progress)
- **Initiative:** [Name]
- **Goal:** [Outcome sought]
- **Status:** [Building/Testing]

## NEXT (Upcoming)
- **Problem:** [Pain point to solve]
- **Hypothesis:** [Proposed solution]

## LATER (Exploring)
- **Theme:** [Strategic area]
- **Objective:** [Long-term goal]
```
