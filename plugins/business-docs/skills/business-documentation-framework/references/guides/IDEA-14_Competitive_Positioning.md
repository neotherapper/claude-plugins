# Generation Guide: IDEA-14 Competitive Positioning

**Purpose:** Visually represent where you sit relative to competitors and identify strategic whitespace.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-12 Porter's Five Forces

## Questions to Ask the User
- What are the two most important axes for your customers (e.g., Price vs Power)?
- Who are your top 3-5 direct competitors?
- Where do you lead, and where do you trail?
