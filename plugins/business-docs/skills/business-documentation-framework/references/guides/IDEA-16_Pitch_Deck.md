# Generation Guide: IDEA-16 Pitch Deck (GATEWAY)

**Purpose:** Synthesize all ideation work into a compelling presentation for funding and recruitment.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-10 Business Model Canvas
- IDEA-15 Why Now? Analysis

## Questions to Ask the User
- How much are you raising and at what valuation?
- What is your specific use of funds?
- What is the single most impressive metric/fact about your project so far?

## Document Template

```markdown
# Pitch Deck Outline

**ID:** IDEA-16
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** IDEA-10, IDEA-15
**Gateway:** Yes

---
```
