# Generation Guide: IDEA-06 Vision Statement

**Purpose:** Define the desired future state of the company and the impact it will have at scale.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-05 Mission Statement

## Extraction from IDEA-05
- Core purpose
- Long-term aspirations implied by the mission

## Questions to Ask the User
- What does the world look like in 5-10 years if you succeed?
- What are the major pillars of your long-term success?
- What is the one sentence that represents your ultimate goal?

## Document Template

```markdown
# Vision Statement

**ID:** IDEA-06
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Created:** [Date]
**Dependencies:** IDEA-05
**Gateway:** No

---

## Our Vision
> [One sentence: The future state you're working toward]

## Time Horizon
- **3-5 year vision:** [Medium-term goal]
- **10-year vision:** [Long-term aspiration]

## Vision Pillars
1. [Core pillar 1]
2. [Core pillar 2]
3. [Core pillar 3]

## Success Indicators
- We'll know we're achieving this when: [Measurable long-term outcome]
```
