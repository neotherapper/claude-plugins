# Generation Guide: PROD-10 Business Requirements Document (BRD)

**Purpose:** Capture high-level business needs and stakeholder objectives for large-scale or enterprise initiatives.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-04 Problem Statement
- IDEA-09 Lean Canvas

## Questions to Ask the User
- What are the core business objectives for this project?
- Who are the key internal and external stakeholders?
- What are the major constraints or assumptions?
