# Generation Guide: IDEA-10 Business Model Canvas

**Purpose:** Provide a comprehensive view of the organization's value creation, delivery, and capture mechanisms for scaling.

---

## Prerequisites
- IDEA-09 Lean Canvas

## Extraction from IDEA-09
- Value Proposition
- Customer Segments
- Revenue Streams
- Cost Structure

## Questions to Ask the User
- Who are your key strategic partners?
- What are the core activities required to deliver your value prop?
- What physical, intellectual, or human resources are critical?
